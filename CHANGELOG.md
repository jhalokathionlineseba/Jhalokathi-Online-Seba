# Changelog - ঝালকাঠি অনলাইন সেবা (Jhalakathi Online Seba)

All notable changes to this project will be documented in this file.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-17

### Added
- **Premium Bengali Digital Service Portal**: Built for Jhalakathi district covering Jhalakathi Sadar, Nalchity, Rajapur, and Kathalia.
- **Riverside Hero Banner & Branding**: Authentic JOS logo emblem with Dhanshiri/Sugandha river motif and Gabkhan 5th Friendship Bridge silhouette.
- **Dynamic Service Category Grid**: Health, Ambulance, Blood Donors, Police & Fire, Electricity, Transport, Legal, and Education.
- **Emergency Helpline Directory**: 24/7 Police Control Room, Hospital, Fire Service, Ambulance, and National Helplines (999, 333) with one-click direct calling.
- **Business & Professional Directory**: Verified badge system, upazila and category filtering, call and WhatsApp integration.
- **News, Notices & Circulars Hub**: Administrative circulars, job notices, tourism guides, and health messages with clean popup reader.
- **Advertisement Management System**: Multi-placement ad engine supporting Hero Top, Between Sections, Sidebar, Footer, and Floating Bottom Bar with impression and click analytics.
- **Citizen Service Submission**: Citizen self-service portal to request business listings.
- **Complete Secure Admin Panel**: Multi-tab dashboard, post authoring, directory moderation, emergency contact editor, ad controller, and audit logging.
- **Real-Time Firebase Integration**: Official Firebase modular SDK integration with live listeners and resilient offline/local fallback.
- **Security & Data Deliverables**: Hardened `firestore.rules`, `storage.rules`, `firestore.indexes.json`, `firebase.json`, `firebase-blueprint.json`, and `security_spec.md`.
- **In-App Source Code Exporter**: One-click downloadable source code archive (.ZIP).
